    What it can do

    01

    Arithmetic

    Any expression with the usual operators, parentheses, powers, and roots — evaluated exactly, not approximated when it doesn't need to be.

    02

    Equations

    Linear and quadratic equations get solved algebraically, with the formula shown. Anything else falls back to a numeric root search across a wide range.

    03

    Simplifying

    Expand brackets, combine like terms, and reduce an expression to its simplest algebraic form.

    04

    Derivatives

    Symbolic differentiation for single-variable expressions — polynomials, trig, exponentials, and combinations of them.

    05

    Integrals

    Polynomials are integrated symbolically. Give bounds ("from a to b") and anything else gets a numeric definite integral instead.

    06

    Plots

    Ask it to plot a function of x and it draws the curve on a set of axes, right in the page.

    It doesn't attempt proofs, word problems, multi-variable systems, or anything past first-year calculus — it's built for the everyday algebra-and-calculus category, worked out honestly rather than guessed at.

    Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
